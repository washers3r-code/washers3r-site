# Washers3R — promo video storylines

**The one thing:** Washers3R comes to your door and gets your bike ready to ride. No 3–5 day wait at the shop.
**What viewers should remember:** "I don't have to drop my bike off anymore. They come to me."
**Sources:** the site's copy (index.html), the founder's note, the 5 gallery photos, the W3R marble/gold logo and the card ("Wait less, ride more").

---

## A. « 3 à 5 jours » (recommended: it tells Gabriel's real story, and the pain is one everybody knows)

- **Device / form:** life before and after, told as a match cut on one shape: the wheel.
- **What you see:** a shop's "ready in 3–5 days" ticket. The days tick by on a wall calendar and the bike stays stuck. Then the same wheel shape turns into a Washers3R wheel rolling up to a door. The real finished bikes from the gallery come by one after another, the W3R gold ring closes around the last wheel, and the video ends on "Wait less, ride more."
- **First 2 s:** big type on cream: « Prêt dans **3 à 5 jours** ». A shop ticket stamps onto the frame, and its "5" starts flipping 1→2→3…
- **Beats:** ticket hook → calendar days flip, bike waits (old way) → « On se déplace chez vous » (wheel match cut) → 3 steps: *Réservez · On débarque · Vous roulez* → gallery payoff: 5 real bikes, each "ready" → « Plus de temps à rouler » → W3R gold ring end card + « Réserver · washers3r »
- **Look:** cream ground and Space Grotesk headlines from the site. Black marble with gold lines only for the end card, so the brand lands once. Gold accent used on a single word per line.
- **Sound:** upbeat acoustic/indie loop (free library). A ticket stamp, a calendar flip, a freewheel click on the match cut, and a bell ding on the end card.

## B. « Toujours prêt à rouler »

- **Device / form:** the result first, cut on the beat.
- **What you see:** hard cuts on the music through the 5 real bikes, each one a full-bleed photo. Kinetic type punches in over them: *Nettoyé. Graissé. Ajusté. Prêt.* Then the 5 MPV prices (35 $ → 160 $) slam in as a staircase, and the end card lands.
- **First 2 s:** the green Norco on the night basketball court fills the frame. « NETTOYÉ » hits on the downbeat.
- **Beats:** bike + verb ×4 → « À votre porte » → MPV 01–05 price staircase → « Wait less, ride more » → logo
- **Look:** full-bleed photos, giant white Space Grotesk type, and a gold bar that wipes on every cut.
- **Sound:** a punchy drum-led track with the cuts on the kick.

## C. « La piste » (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** a character does the work, in one continuous take.
- **What you see:** the camera follows Gabriel's real BMX jump photo. The rider lands, and the "track" becomes a line drawn across a map of Trois-Rivières. The line passes houses (pins), and at each stop a gallery bike pops up clean. It ends at the BMX3R track for the clinic (dimanche 27 septembre, 300 rue Chapeleau) and then the logo.
- **First 2 s:** the BMX jump photo, frozen mid-air, with « Passionné de vélo depuis toujours ».
- **Beats:** jump → founder line → route across town → bikes at each stop → BMX3R clinic → end card
- **Look:** a flat map in cream and ink, a gold route line and photo cut-outs.
- **Sound:** a driving electronic beat and a whoosh on the route.
- **Note:** the clinic date may already be past (the site lists Sept 27), so this one is more dated than A or B.
